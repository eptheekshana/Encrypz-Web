About Us

Our Mission

At Encrypz, we believe that privacy is a fundamental human right. In an era where data is commodified and mass surveillance is commonplace, we set out to build tools that give control back to the users.

The Problem with the Cloud

Cloud storage is incredibly convenient, but it requires you to trust a third party with your most sensitive information. We realized that the only way to truly secure data in the cloud is to ensure it is encrypted *before* it leaves your device.

Our Approach

We built Encrypz to be seamless, requiring no complicated desktop installations. By leveraging modern Web Crypto APIs, we've created a powerful encryption engine that runs entirely in your browser. You bring your own storage, and we provide the lock and key.

Open Source

Trust is earned through transparency. That's why our core cryptographic implementation is open source. We invite the community to audit our code and contribute to a more secure future for everyone.
