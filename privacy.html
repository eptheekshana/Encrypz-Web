Privacy Policy

1. Zero-Knowledge Architecture

Encrypz is built from the ground up on a zero-knowledge architecture. This means that all encryption and decryption processes happen entirely within your local web browser. We never transmit, store, or have access to your master password or the unencrypted contents of your files.

2. Data Collection

We believe in collecting the absolute minimum amount of data required to provide our service. We do not track your IP address, use tracking cookies, or employ third-party analytics software that compromises your privacy.

3. Cloud Providers

When you connect Encrypz to a third-party cloud provider (like Google Drive or OneDrive), the authorization token is stored locally on your device. We do not proxy your files through our servers; your encrypted data streams directly between your browser and your cloud provider.

4. Open Source Transparency

Our cryptographic core is completely open-source. We encourage security researchers and our users to audit our code to independently verify our privacy claims.
