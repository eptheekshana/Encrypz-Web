Cloud Storage is Convenient. It Isn't Private.

Traditional cloud storage leaves your most sensitive data vulnerable to scanning, breaches, and third-party access.

They Hold the Keys

Cloud providers encrypt your data on their servers, meaning they hold the decryption keys and can scan, analyze, or hand over your files at any time.

Centralized Breaches

When cloud servers are breached, your plaintext data is compromised. Relying on provider-side security creates a massive single point of failure.

Complex, Clunky Tools

Traditional encryption software requires heavy desktop installs, complicated key management, or forcing you to move away from your existing cloud ecosystem.

Your Privacy, Guaranteed.

Encrypz bridges the gap between the convenience of modern cloud storage and the absolute security of local cryptography.

What We Do For You

We seamlessly encrypt your files inside your browser before they are ever uploaded to your cloud provider. You get a secure, organized vault without needing to install complex desktop software.

Why It Matters

Cloud breaches and unauthorized data scanning happen every day. By encrypting your data locally, even if your cloud provider is completely compromised, your files remain mathematically unreadable to hackers, corporations, and governments.

The Zero-Trust Model

In a zero-trust architecture, you don't need to trust the cloud provider, the network, or even us. You only need to trust the math.

Security That Never Leaves Your Device

Advanced cryptography executed entirely within your browser, putting you in absolute control.

100% Zero-Knowledge

We never see your password, keys, or files. Cryptographic execution happens entirely on your local machine, in-browser.

AES-256-GCM Encryption

Dynamic in-memory encryption that protects data confidentiality and instantly detects tampering through authenticated encryption.

Argon2id Key Derivation

Computational passphrase stretching that transforms simple master passwords into uncrackable cryptographic keys resisting GPU attacks.

Native Cloud Integration

Direct OAuth integration with Google Drive and OneDrive without middleman backend servers reading your data in transit.

Large File Streaming

Powered by the Web Streams API to encrypt gigabyte-sized files smoothly in browser memory without crashing or lagging.

Zero Downloads Required

Fully browser-based. Access your encrypted vault instantly from any modern web browser without installing any heavy desktop clients.

Effortless Privacy in Three Simple Steps

We handle the complex cryptography so you can focus on getting work done.

1

Connect

Authenticate directly with your cloud provider (e.g., Google Drive) via secure OAuth 2.0. No middleman involved.

2

Lock

Set your master password. Encrypz derives your local keys using Argon2id in a background Web Worker, ensuring main thread performance.

3

Encrypt & Sync

Drop files into your vault. Encrypz chunks, encrypts, and obfuscates filenames locally before streaming ciphertext directly to your cloud.

Open Source & Client-Side Verification

Trust is earned, not given. Encrypz stores zero data on our servers. Our core cryptographic engine is fully open-source for community auditing, and all security operations rely exclusively on standard Web Crypto API primitives provided directly by your browser.
